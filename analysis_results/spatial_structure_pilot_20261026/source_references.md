# Source references and provenance

- DR1 galaxy products: Baes et al.2024, A&A683,A181, DOI [10.1051/0004-6361/202348418](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202348418). Local cached spatial images, not integrated-photometry replacements. The cache input hashes are recorded in input_provenance.json.
- Existing audited design calculator: [ULTIMATE Subaru sensitivity calculator](https://github.com/sali131/ULTIMATE-Subaru-Sensitivity-Calculator). This pilot reuses the previously audited local code/data in screen_test_20261026, including its corrections for electron conversion and exposure splitting. It does not independently verify that provisional design parameters describe a final instrument. See the earlier instrument_audit.md and the copied instrument_parameters.csv.
- Static radiometric principles: the previously inspected [forward-modelling repository](https://github.com/physicsbahare/astro-forward-modeling), preserved in the prior experiment's provenance/report. Reuse of cosmological distances, specific-intensity dimming, photon conversion and area-overlap resampling; no Sérsic substitution or intrinsic evolution.
- Gaussian PSF homogenization in this pilot is analytic positive convolution. It does not assume that Gaussian matching is valid for arbitrary empirical or Moffat PSFs; the Moffat test is restricted to native Ks. Empirical PSF work would require verified kernels and propagated covariance.
- SDSS DR17 documentation: individual supporting links and proposed selection/masking assumptions are given in MaNGA_validation_note.md, consulted2026-10-26. No MaNGA data downloaded. It is not assumed that nebular and stellar attenuation are identical.
- Earlier numerical false-positive and physical-model failures: scientific_report.md in screen_test_20261026, dr1_calibrated_screen_20261026 and multiplicative_diagnosis_20261026; all preserved by this task's hash manifest.
