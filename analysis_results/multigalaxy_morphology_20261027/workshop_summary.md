# Workshop summary: dust changes morphology; resolution benefits depend on the measurement

Executed: 26 galaxies × 5 correlated views × 22 paired bands. Fixed-rest-band observing comparison, not an exact ULTIMATE forecast.

- LSST_g: median galaxy-mean ΔR50 0.400 kpc [0.170, 0.546], galaxy-bootstrap 95% interval; signs and individual ranges in Figure 03.
- 2MASS_Ks: median galaxy-mean ΔR50 0.124 kpc [0.083, 0.170], galaxy-bootstrap 95% interval; signs and individual ranges in Figure 03.

Suggested four-slide sequence:
1. Figure 02: wavelength dependence and honest sample scatter.
2. Figure 03: each galaxy is one sampling unit; show full viewing-angle ranges.
3. Figure 05: distinguish morphology preservation from dust-difference recovery. Gains are conditional on the provisional PSF/noise scenario.
4. Figure 09: show favorable, unfavorable and weak-dust examples; no unique dust-feature claim.

Keep Figure 01 (selection), Figure 07 (aperture/center sensitivity), and Figure 10 (PSF/depth sensitivity) as backup. The prior geometry-inference failures motivate this measurable counterfactual question; they are not hidden.

Caveats on the slides: balanced 26-object feasibility sample; R<12-kpc sizes; correlated directions; clear images are simulation truth; z=0 fixed-rest-band degradation; rest Ks is not observer Ks at z=0.1; provisional PSFs/backgrounds; no calibrated performance promise or dust mass/geometry recovery.

Prior work already covers atlas dust-dependent nonparametric morphology: Bokona Tulu et al. (2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20886. Distinctive paper value must come from calibrated instrumental transfer and error recovery, with additional independent validation.

The zero-projected-dust source-product residuals must be resolved before claiming small paired differences as physical dust signals. Raw/noise-corrected asymmetry also gives conflicting preservation gains.

Paper path: strengthen aperture and measurement-systematic control, use validated observer-filter synthesis and empirical PSFs/sky, and test independent observational morphology. Current results support a workshop and a conditional simulation-study draft, with these gaps explicit.
