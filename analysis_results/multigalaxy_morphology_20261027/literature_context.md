# Bounded publication-positioning check, 2026-10-27

Read primary source abstract and relevant methods text; no science data acquired.

- [Baes et al. 2024, DR1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.04224): z=0 matched image/map atlas. Actual available FITS headers and filters govern this experiment.
- [Pastrav et al. 2013](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.5602): radiative-transfer effects on fitted disk/bulge structural parameters. Generic dust-induced morphology changes are established prior work.
- [Bokona Tulu et al. 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20886), [methods](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20886v1): larger atlas study of wavelength/dust-dependent nonparametric morphology. StatMorph/segmentation/Petrosian-aperture measurements differ from our fixed-origin aperture protocol. The authors identify O4/O5 as antipodal and report asymmetry instability. A four-view sensitivity check here excludes O5 while preserving the primary five-view analysis.

This is enough to reject novelty based solely on studying dust-induced morphology in 26 galaxies. Distinctive value must come from calibrated instrumental transfer, explicit measurement limitations and independent observational tests. It is not an exhaustive literature review. No DR2 route investigation or new simulation dataset download was performed.
