# Empirical sensitivity models — scope and use

These are HAWK-I/GRAAL reduced-image sensitivity inputs. They have not been injected into mocks or used to train/evaluate ML. They measure neither dust truth nor ULTIMATE performance.

* `joint_PSF_parameter_samples.csv`: preserve FWHM, q, beta and orientation together; independent marginal draws can create unobserved combinations. Use spatial cells/fields as sampling units when exploring PSF variation. Select `wing_reliable` rows for more isolated empirical wings; detector2 has insufficient strict-wing coverage. Sky PA is degrees east of north; theta is in original image pixel axes.
* `positive_Moffat_PSF_kernels.npz`: 15 positive, unit-sum 129-pixel analytic templates at observed sampling. Width quantiles select real fitted parameter tuples. The manifest states support and fallback selection. Beyond the measured 2-arcsec growth aperture, these are model extrapolations. Kernels are centre-sampled analytic approximations; a later renderer must integrate on its own pixel grid, rather than repeatedly resampling these arrays. Do not infer an optical PSF by unconstrained deconvolution of their ~2.8-pixel cores.
* `finite_growth_core_halo_kernels.npz` and `finite_growth_model_grid.csv`: nine-subpixel-integrated Moffat cores plus a nonnegative Gaussian halo, fitted to the finite R2 growth medians. These stress templates address the measured outer-light excess over a single Moffat. Their full-grid halo fraction and width are degenerate fitted assumptions, not physical scattered-light measurements. The grid preserves all halo widths and alternatives within0.01 EE RMS of the optimum; it is not an independent validation. Detector2 has only four clean stars and its optimum hits the3-arcsec width boundary: use as a poorly constrained sensitivity only. Residual crowding and sky errors can mimic a halo. The model fits leave all original star measurements intact.
* Empirical growth curves in `../empirical_profiles.csv` retain signed sky subtraction, finite-aperture references and contamination flags in `../science_star_table.csv`. Use them to check analytic cores/wings; they are not isolated infinite-radius PSFs.
* `local_covariance_kernels.npz` / `local_noise_family.json`: unit-L2 nonnegative filters acting on white Gaussian fields. They reproduce the observed adjacent-pixel correlations; their aperture predictions are tested against independent numerical realizations. They intentionally do not account for all large-aperture excess.
* `noise_family.json`: sums of independent, unit-L2 Gaussian-filtered fields with sqrt(variance_fraction) amplitudes. The covariance is positive semidefinite. The fits approximate total reduced-background correlations and R2–16 aperture responses; inspect `noise_response_validation.csv` and retain the 13–37% maximum response discrepancies. They include unresolved stellar light and sky residuals; they are not uniquely calibrated instrumental noise. No R32 constraint was accepted.

Recommended later experiments: (1) local covariance only; (2) the broader total-background stress family; (3) source-mask-growth/faint-catalogue stress envelopes. Match pixel scale and observed correlation length in angular units; retest the aperture response after resampling. Set noise amplitude from the mock observing/exposure model, not from these ADU values. Avoid adding a full-background mixture and a second correlated kernel to the same noise realization without explicitly accounting for double convolution. Add source Poisson noise separately using the future instrument model.

Keep actual observed ~0.29–0.32 arcsec HAWK-I cores separate from any hypothetical 0.2 arcsec ULTIMATE case. A hypothetical narrower PSF must be labelled an assumed instrumental sensitivity, not an empirical measurement. Hold optical g at its seeing-limited specification. Preserve the completed ML freeze; any future confirmatory ML study needs genuinely unused data or an explicitly exploratory designation.
