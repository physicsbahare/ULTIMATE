# Source-pair label reliability audit — 2026-10-28

The source-product mismatch is confirmed, while its exact physical/numerical cause remains unresolved. Operational signed clear/dusty pair labels can be modelled as such. Small predicted differences cannot yet be presented as calibrated physical dust attenuation or morphology corrections. No expanded-sample images, new validation labels, or final-20 scientific data were opened for this audit.

## Scope and reproducibility

Only the four previously analysed zero projected-dust-field controls (13, 167418, 184937, 613192), their old caches, old FITS inventories, and original old archives were used. `audit_old_controls.py` independently reads original O1 g/J/Ks pairs plus dust maps using a minimal primary-HDU FITS decoder; all five old views and 22 bands are analysed through the earlier full-field FITS sums. `make_old_quality_masks.py` produces descriptive flags for all 130 old galaxy/view rows and seven old targets (910 flags). Run both with **python3** from the workspace. Old files remain read-only.

## Direct source versus processing checks

All 24 freshly decoded O1 broadband images exactly equal their cached 400×400 central crop at source pixels [600:1000,600:1000]. Their full-field sums agree with the existing inventories to <=3.64e-12 in summed pixel intensity. Both scenes use MJy/sr, 1600×1600 pixels, 100 pc pixels and the same reference coordinates. All four independently decoded O1 dust maps have zero nonzero pixels. See `raw_cache_checks.csv` and `raw_headers.json`. This rules out a local cache crop, precision conversion, or pair-unit mismatch in these checked files as the origin of the effect. It does not prove every archive member is physically calibrated.

The full 160-kpc field preserves the offset: g median +0.120994 mag (range +0.117605 to +0.123231; sample SD across 20 galaxy/views 0.001974); Ks +0.019342 mag (+0.014892 to +0.021350; SD 0.001853). This excludes a fixed R12 aperture boundary as the dominant integrated-offset cause. Views of the same four galaxies are correlated and are not 20 independent null experiments.

The band pattern is strongly non-monotonic and changes sign: full-field medians are FUV −0.152646, Johnson B −0.029466, g +0.120994, r −0.023176, i +0.110677, z −0.052064, Euclid VIS −0.069878 and WISE W2 +0.102418 mag. Every one of the 22 bands keeps its own sign over all 20 views; see `cross_band_summary.csv` and `old_control_fullfield_ratios.csv`. This is inconsistent with explaining the observations as a simple extinction-law attenuation of a common source SED. Scattering can yield local or directional brightening, but a truly dust-free transfer volume should not generate such an extinction signal; a zero projected dust map alone does not establish the entire source model and transfer volume.

After dividing dusty images by their R12 flux ratio, spatial residual RMS relative to clear RMS falls under 1-kpc-FWHM smoothing: g 0.01806→0.00564, J 0.01839→0.00477, Ks 0.02030→0.00694. This supports a high-frequency spatial component compatible with Monte Carlo noise. The quoted amplitude normalization is intentionally fixed before smoothing: its unchanged value in `spatial_summary.csv` is by construction, not an independent flux-conservation test. Full-field ratios provide the independent integrated check. Smoothing suppresses the spatial component by factors 3.2, 3.9 and 2.9 but leaves a nonzero residual, which may include source projection/spectral-population effects.

## What the original methodology and official software establish

DR1 uses BC03/Chabrier sources for stars older than 10 Myr, MAPPINGS III templates for younger particles, and 10^9 photon packets. Dusty images are broadband Monte Carlo instrument outputs; clear images are analytic emissivity projections with no Monte Carlo noise. The original 18 bands were later supplemented in the local archive by four Euclid bands (22 observed pairs per view). The paper does not give the historical probe convolution setting or a filter-version checksum. [Baes et al. 2024, A&A 683 A181, sections 2.4 and 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.04224v1).

Current official SKIRT documentation exposes a concrete operator distinction: `ImportedSourceLuminosityProbe` with `convolve=false` samples the SED at the bin characteristic wavelength; `convolve=true` convolves with the filter. Its current default is false. [Official probe class](https://skirt.ugent.be/skirt9/class_imported_source_luminosity_probe.html). For a band grid the characteristic wavelength is the pivot. [Official grid class](https://skirt.ugent.be/skirt9/class_band_wavelength_grid.html). Monte Carlo instruments weight each photon by the band transmission. [Official wavelength configuration](https://skirt.ugent.be/root/_user_wave_configure.html).

The documented analytic band quantity is <L_lambda>=integral[L_lambda T dlambda]/integral[T dlambda], and lambda_pivot²=integral[T dlambda]/integral[T/lambda² dlambda]. Photon-counter response R requires T=lambda R before use. [Official Band definition](https://skirt.ugent.be/skirt9/class_band.html). Thus monochromatic-versus-band integration, response weighting, or filter versions can cause SED-dependent offsets even with matching BUNIT. These are **candidate mechanisms**, not proven DR1 configuration errors. No original .ski, source particle SEDs, historical filter tables/version, or packet reliability statistics were found locally; attempted public raw-source-code fetches failed, while official generated class documentation was accessible. Current documentation cannot establish the historical DR1 executable defaults.

## Competing mechanisms and strength of evidence

| Mechanism | Audit implication | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Local crop, unit or cache-processing error | 24 exact raw/cache matches; equal pair units/WCS; full-field offset persists | Disfavoured for checked O1 g/J/Ks pairs |
| Pure zero-mean Monte Carlo spatial noise | Strong smoothing response, but coherent ~11% g flux deficit and sign-stable band pattern across galaxies/views | Explains some spatial residual; insufficient explanation of integrated pattern alone |
| Different analytic-probe/RT spectral operators | Documented monochromatic/convolved switch; alternating band signs and galaxy-dependent amplitudes compatible | Specific leading hypothesis; historical setting unknown |
| Spectral quadrature/filter versions or response convention | Matching band names/units do not establish same throughput operator | Plausible, untested without original config/SED/filter resources |
| Different spatial projection/smoothing kernels | Can leave structured normalized residuals and aperture-dependent morphology | Plausible contributor; full-field offset makes it unlikely to explain integrated signal alone |
| Birth-cloud source SED or residual dust outside mapped field | Projected zero does not establish all source-template/volume components; exact stellar inputs absent | Not excluded; band pattern does not support simple uniform extinction |

The smallest discriminating next experiment is a source-owner reproduction on one old null model: freeze the particle/source SEDs and historical filters, generate analytic probes with convolve both false and true, run NoMedium instruments with repeated seeds and increased photon counts, and compare full-field and matched apertures. A convergence series quantifies Monte Carlo variance; direct spectral integration isolates a deterministic operator bias. No new large-data download or new final-label access is needed to formulate this experiment.

## Label uncertainty and reproducible masks

Keep signed operational labels without subtracting an empirical common offset or clipping negative values. `quality_policy.json` and `old_fixed12_quality_flags.csv` record finite-label, source-null, aperture-limited and within-old-control-envelope flags separately. The envelope is max absolute residual from four old galaxies, **not** sigma, confidence coverage, a physical detection threshold, or an automatic exclusion rule. Native fixed-R12 maximum absolute scales are 0.123917 mag in g, 0.021591 mag in Ks, 0.118867 mag for smoothed pixel p50 g attenuation, 0.031089/0.039185 kpc for g/Ks dR50 and 0.069286/0.045964 for g/Ks dC. These are values from the earlier target-specific audit, retained without relabelling truth.

For expanded training, require matched band, shape, WCS, units, finite nonnegative intensities and positive aperture sums. Apply the same aperture selected from observed dusty Ks to both scenes, record truncation/clipping and retain fixed R12 sensitivity. The proposed R_ap=clip(1.5 R80 within R<35 kpc,8,30 kpc) is observationally implementable, but its R80 is relative to a finite 35-kpc flux reference; do not call it a total-light radius without testing. R12 control envelopes do not calibrate adaptive-aperture/common-seeing labels. Remeasure old controls with the exact final operator, then freeze those descriptive thresholds before any new validation/final labels are accessed. Physically calibrated correction claims remain withheld even when operational prediction is accurate.
