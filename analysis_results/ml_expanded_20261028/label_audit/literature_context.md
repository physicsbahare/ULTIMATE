# Bounded primary-literature context — checked 2026-10-28

This comparison supports a focused feasibility claim; it is not an exhaustive novelty search. No paper below establishes observational truth for the exact paired g/Ks morphology-difference targets here.

| Verified work | Inputs, target and validation | Implication and limits for this project |
|---|---|---|
| **Fu et al. 2025**, *Dust extinction measures for z~8 galaxies using machine learning on JWST imaging*, MNRAS 540, 2081–2097, DOI 10.1093/mnras/staf836 | IllustrisTNG high-z mock normalized 32×32 JWST F277W alone or F277W/F356W/F444W images; CNNs estimate E(B−V), A_V and sSFR. S/N≥5 retains 6620 of 33376 images, augmented to 25208; reported 85/15 train/test split. Applied to CEERS/JADES galaxies and compared with Bagpipes. | Direct precedent for image-based attenuation learning. Published text does not establish an object-grouped split before augmentation; do not assume independent-galaxy performance. Bagpipes comparison is model-dependent rather than direct dust truth. Different epoch, bands and targets; it does not validate z=0 DR1 pair morphology corrections. [Published primary paper](https://academic.oup.com/mnras/article/540/3/2081/8140852); [2024 original preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.18458). |
| **Dobbels & Baes 2021**, *Predicting far-infrared maps of galaxies via machine learning techniques*, A&A 655, A34, DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202142084 | Nearby DustPedia galaxies plus M31/M33; UV–MIR surface-brightness inputs to a per-pixel random forest; resolved specific dust luminosity, specific dust mass and temperature targets, pixel scales 150 pc–3kpc. Abstract reports galaxy-to-galaxy bias accounting for about two-thirds of scatter. | The often-mentioned “Dobbels 2022” could not be uniquely verified. This closest resolved-dust ML paper is **2021**, not 2022. Strong motivation for galaxy-grouped testing and photometry baselines; targets concern dust emission/SED-derived properties rather than paired dust-induced morphology differences. Full split/label-building details were not verified in this bounded review; do not invent them. [Primary preprint with journal metadata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.01704); [publisher](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202142084). |
| **Bokona Tulu et al. 2026**, *The TNG50-SKIRT Atlas: Multi-wavelength nonparametric galaxy morphology*, arXiv:2605.20886 | Same 1154-galaxy z=0 TSA population; StatMorph C,Gini,M20,bulge statistic; Gaussian PSF sigma 3 pixels, Gaussian background peak S/N 100, crop 8 stellar half-mass radii, morphology-quality/SN/size cuts, 4329 image sets after selection. Dust effects studied directly; asymmetry/smoothness excluded as unstable under MC noise. | Direct overlap in dust/wavelength morphology science: no broad novelty claim that TSA dust-induced morphology has never been analysed. Opportunity is a controlled observational-input predictor of signed pair biases with grouped validation and label/operator uncertainty. Their morphology operator differs from our fixed/adaptive apertures. [Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.20886v1). |
| **Ćiprijanović et al. 2021**, *DeepMerge II: Building Robust Deep Learning Algorithms for Merging Galaxy Identification Across Domains*, arXiv:2103.01373 | Merger classification using MMD and domain-adversarial learning; Illustris-1→Illustris-1 domains and nearby Illustris-1→SDSS transfer. | Explicit sim-to-real precedent. Classification success and domain alignment do not establish calibration of a continuous attenuation/correction target. Our within-TSA held-out accuracy remains a same-simulator result. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.01373). |
| **Garuda et al. 2026**, *HaloFlow II: Robust Galaxy Halo Mass Inference with Domain Adaptation*, arXiv:2603.12380 | Galaxy-image halo-mass inference; cross-simulation TNG/EAGLE/SIMBA tests; DANN/MMD reduce bias and improve posterior calibration. | Strong recent evidence that even precise same-simulation predictions can be biased/overconfident across galaxy-formation physics. Target is halo mass, not dust; cross-simulation validation is distinct from real-dust ground truth. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.12380). |

An additional boundary is [Baes et al. 2024b, A&A 683, A182](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/202348419), which already studies effective-radius wavelength and dust effects (arXiv:2401.04225). This continuation is restricted to the downloaded DR1 products.

Recommended claim: a bounded study of whether observed photometry/morphology predicts **operational DR1 paired-image differences**, beyond photometry-only and constant baselines, on entirely held-out galaxies. Separate this from physical dust recovery, instrument transfer, sim-to-real validity and novelty of measuring dust-induced morphology changes. Literature supports multi-band inputs, grouped object splits, strong simple baselines, MC-aware quality cuts and explicit domain-shift tests; it does not justify treating synthetic pair labels as exact physical calibration.
