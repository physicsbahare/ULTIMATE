# Instrument and noise provenance
Uses read-only `../screen_test_20261026/instrument.py` and `forward_physics.py`, whose pinned sources and prior audit remain preserved. Source calculator commit [2187748a40d4207519a8eff0d2a5b6701db8da0d](https://github.com/sali131/ULTIMATE-Subaru-Sensitivity-Calculator/tree/2187748a40d4207519a8eff0d2a5b6701db8da0d); forward-method commit [78dce5ad96038fe7a702d3e2f2b434a2c7d7e8af](https://github.com/physicsbahare/astro-forward-modeling/tree/78dce5ad96038fe7a702d3e2f2b434a2c7d7e8af). Structured SKIRT scenes replace any smooth parametric morphology.

- Area pi*(8.0819m/2)^2*(1-.277²), pixel .1arcsec. Source electrons/s = A*1e-26/h*integral(Fnu[Jy]*T(lambda)/lambda d_lambda); intensity becomes Jy per source pixel through its solid angle. A flat-Fnu nominal-band conversion is used for the DR1 band average; it is not a resolved SED reconstruction.
- NIR design assumptions: efficiency .45*.95*.98*atmosphere, local Mauna Kea atmosphere/sky PWV1.6mm and airmass1, calculator sky/moon/thermal including telescope emission. Original sky/moon area convention corrected for obscuration consistently with prior audit. RN16e; dark .01e/s/pixel provisional. Optical throughput, sky AB22/21/20.3/19.5 mag/arcsec², RN5e and dark .001 are analyst scenarios.
- Main exposure is 1h per band, seven bands =7 on-source hours before overheads. Development sensitivities .25 and4h/band. DIT60s, respectively15/60/240 reads. Variance = (source+sky+thermal+moon+dark)*T + Nread*RN². Known mean sky is subtracted. Operating DIT/full-well, nonlinearity, persistence, correlated sky residuals, dither coverage and flat fields remain unverified; H background can reach ~58ke/pixel/read. These are NOT a validated observing prescription.
- The full aperture covariance is W diag(variance) W^T, including covariance introduced by PSF homogenization. Compressed Gaussian draws use these exact first/second moments, not zero-source noise. A separate 1000-draw exact Poisson+summed-read comparison tests two weighted apertures; a 10000-draw test checks normalization bias/covariance at low-signal configurations. These bounded checks do not validate unknown instrumental systematics.
- Primary PSFs are normalized Gaussians, optical .7arcsec for both modes. NIR uses published calculator median D50 values (for Gaussians D50=FWHM): J~.658/.508, H~.624/.446, Ks~.588/.390arcsec seeing/GLAO. No GLAO PSF is assigned to g,r,i,z. Common matched Gaussian target .8arcsec. Native strategy applies each band's own PSF to the latent covariance before aperture compression; no common optical smoothing is applied.
- Moffat sensitivity has beta2.5 and **equal D50**, not equal FWHM. Its actual FWHM/D50=.737519. The optical .7arcsec number in this sensitivity is thus D50; no improvement of original images by convolution is asserted. No Moffat matched-strategy result is used in the main experiment.
- Existing HAWK-I raw compact-source fits (~.258-.417arcsec frame medians) remain provisional empirical checks, not validated ULTIMATE PSFs or calibrated sky backgrounds. HAWK-I is unchanged and does not supply new reduction/calibration information in this experiment.
- Shared standard normal deviates pair seeing/GLAO/control simulations, transformed by each scenario's correct photometric covariance. This is common-random-number pairing of conditional simulations, not a claim that different real detector exposures have identical Poisson counts.

All instrument values are design scenarios or provisional analyst assumptions unless specifically documented in the prior pinned audit. See `instrument_parameters.csv` and the bandpass/redshift limitations; especially the shifted-rest-Ks issue at z=.3.
