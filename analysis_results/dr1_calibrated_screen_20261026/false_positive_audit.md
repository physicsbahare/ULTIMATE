# Scientific audit of the original false-positive inference

The earlier calculation is numerically consistent but its physical null is not calibrated. At z=.6, a refined-grid O1 uniform-screen control gave ln B(variable/uniform) ~+8.7 with the restricted population prior, and ~-2.9 with broad templates. Refining the attenuation grid did not repair it. Its population library contained five low-Av effective templates in the restricted case, versus all12 templates and extra mixtures in the broad case. The EAZY `noIRdust` name removes dust emission, not stellar attenuation: broad templates include effective Av up to ~2.94. Both fits then add another screen. Template reddening is therefore entangled with the fitted screen, and discrete equal template weights depend on the chosen library/mixture enumeration.

Every aperture previously had its own discrete population choice and amplitude, with independent attenuation in the alternative. There was no physically regularized spatial population model or explicit SFH distribution. The 5% discrepancy floor was assumed; changing it to2%/10% changes the result. Selection on measured r/Ks SNR and spectral interpolation add further limitations. Three noise draws per old condition did not calibrate a false-positive rate. Old ln B values are not detection significances.

## New inference and its scope
The new fits never accept an evaluation dust-free SED, physical map, true attenuation, or clear aperture template. The `prepare_data` API retains a legacy `means` argument solely for compatibility but deliberately does not use it; a unit test verifies this. Observed aperture fluxes determine normalization and its Jacobian. Simulated photometric covariance is supplied as a known noise model, including expected source Poisson variance; it is not a stellar template. An errors-in-noise-model approximation remains, as in standard forecasting.

A single training block x=-14..-4kpc, |y|<10kpc supplies one-kpc dust-free *training-only* colours. Evaluation aperture centers are x=4,7,10kpc and y=-6,-3,0,3,6kpc, fixed independently of flux. No evaluation SNR selection or intrinsic counterpart fitting is used. Training data are from the same galaxy, not an independent population sample. Physical age/metallicity/mass maps are read only in post-fit diagnostics.

The hierarchy is (i) common morphology without spatial population-colour covariance; (ii) first empirical population PC; (iii) two PCs plus the measured correlated residual covariance. The latter equals the full measured seven-band covariance. All use a fixed regularized spatial field (3kpc node spacing,1.8kpc RBF width), not unconstrained aperture-by-aperture populations. Colour-field coefficients are Gaussian nuisance priors; they are not recovered ages, metallicities or nonparametric SFHs. Population-scale factors .5/1/2 and law slopes .7/1/1.3 are development robustness tests, not selected to force control acceptance.

Data are linear per-band normalized flux contrasts, with full delta-method covariance. Removing each band mean discards global colour information conservatively: total uniform E and absolute stellar SED are unidentifiable and not estimated. A broad gray morphology field is shared across bands. The null adds empirical population fields; the alternative adds an independent dust-law colour field with the same spatial regularization. Both are Gaussian-marginalized; the statistic is 2*log marginal-likelihood ratio. The alternative is a **linear dust-colour-field approximation**, lacking exact multiplicative morphology weighting. Its posterior amplitudes are flux contrasts, not measurements of physical E or3D dust geometry. Its priors can allow negative intensity fluctuations, another reason to inspect posterior predictions and calibration rather than interpret every coefficient physically.

Each band's PSF is projected into the covariance. Intrinsic field normalization is frozen across modes and subsets; initial condition-dependent normalization was corrected before final execution. Posterior checks draw the latent field from its posterior and compare observed and replicated noise residuals conditional on that SAME latent draw. The initial plug-in check was corrected; all final outputs use the corrected method.

## Calibration lesson retained rather than concealed
The first matched-scene calibration uses five known nulls and separate200 calibration/200 evaluation draws each. It controls errors on those scenes but fails on some independently introduced population-colour perturbations at z=.3. These stress results are retained as development evidence. They were not used to alter the fitted population prior.

The final experiment follows `FINAL_PROTOCOL.md`: freeze the statistic and priors, expand the calibration null to a bounded, training-derived population ensemble, then use500 NEW population/noise draws per null/alternative to evaluate it. This repairs the too-narrow *conditional* null calibration if its held-out rates pass; it does not establish an astrophysical SFH prior or galaxy-to-galaxy robustness. A calibrated large power on a synthetic screen is not by itself evidence that real SKIRT radiative transfer is adequately represented.
