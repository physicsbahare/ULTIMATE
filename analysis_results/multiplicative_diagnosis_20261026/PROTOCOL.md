# Targeted diagnosis and multiplicative-model protocol
Previous experiments remain read-only. No full-grid rerun, downloads, installation, DR2 work or ML. Reproduce only the named catastrophic case with its original seeds and four PSF strategies; deterministic one-assumption-at-a-time diagnosis.

## One new model
Positive luminosity field times a convex mixture of seven independent TRAINING-region DR1 stellar-source SEDs times a positive wavelength-dependent foreground attenuation. Use x<-4kpc training block; no right-side target clear templates or physical maps in inference. Template prototypes are fixed by training spectral extrema along the first three log-colour PCs, plus their mean. These are physically positive empirical broadband mixtures, not a reconstructed SFH/metallicity grid; age-dependent source/birth-cloud uncertainties are not identified separately.

Fit linear observed aperture fluxes with band-specific forward PSF operators and full photometric covariance. Stellar mixture coefficients are spatial fields shared across bands; overall luminosity is nuisance. Model E is uniform or a smoothly varying field with the same stellar model. Native source grid1kpc, positive gray RBF nodes2kpc apart; screen and population fields use generic aperture-grid RBFs. Screen law/observing assumptions remain the previous explicitly provisional matched-rest-band scenario atz=.3. Keep absolute colours in primary likelihood; compare with normalized contrasts using the SAME model and priors.

Independent development patterns: unperturbed DR1 and known problematic anchor. Independently define held-out shifted/rotated gradients, higher training-PC colour modes and compact patches. Do not tune on held-out results. Compare clear, uniform, smooth, patchy and original SKIRT. Use penalized MAP objective improvement as diagnostic statistic (raw chi² also saved), calibrated per scenario rather than interpreting approximate evidence as geometry. Report adequate-fit and rejection fractions separately. Laplace posterior predictive checks are approximate and cannot certify multimodal physical identifiability.

## Gate
First validate adjoint flux operator, derivatives, positivity, nested screen model and synthetic model-family recovery. Development: small noiseless and repeated-noise tests only. Require optimizer convergence and adequate noise-level fits on known-family controls and at least basic physical nulls (posterior-predictive probability >=.01 in prespecified representative fits; effective-dof tail values are descriptive, not calibrated probabilities). If native physical nulls remain inadequate, do not launch a large Monte Carlo or claim a new GLAO performance result. Complete a bounded held-out diagnostic comparison and record the failed gate. GLAO comparison is conditional on adequacy, not on previous8-point gain. A NO-GO with mechanism and missing independent information is acceptable.

Calibration/evaluation seeds, counts, thresholds and actual gate outcomes are saved. Future galaxy-to-galaxy claims remain unavailable with one local galaxy. Larger evaluation, if gate passes, uses independent noise and separate patterns; no scenario averaging may hide failures.
